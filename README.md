# 🚗 CarWash Connect

**CarWash Connect** is a Django-based car wash management platform designed to simplify daily car wash operations.

The system helps car wash businesses manage vehicles, track the live wash queue, notify customers, record payments, monitor revenue, and manage operational activity from one central dashboard.

## Features

- Customer and vehicle registration
- Live car wash queue
- Wash status tracking
- Staff authentication
- Customer WhatsApp notifications
- Payment recording
- Daily revenue reporting
- Car wash operational dashboard
- Platform administration and monitoring
- Multi-car-wash support
- Platform billing monitoring
- Mobile-friendly interface

## User Roles

### Car Wash Staff

Car wash staff can:

- Register customers and vehicles
- Add vehicles to the wash queue
- Update wash progress
- Mark vehicles ready for collection
- Record payments
- Monitor daily operations
- View daily reports

### Platform Administrator

The platform administrator can:

- Monitor connected car washes
- View platform activity
- Monitor completed washes
- Review transaction values
- Monitor platform billing
- View individual car wash activity
